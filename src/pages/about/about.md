# About Walletbeat

Walletbeat is a public good project that brings transparency to the Ethereum wallet ecosystem.

**As [L2Beat](https://l2beat.com) has done for Ethereum Layer 2s, Walletbeat aims to do the same for Ethereum wallets.**

---

Walletbeat aims to be fair, objective and impartial in its assessment methodology of Ethereum wallets.

Where there is inherent subjectivity, such as in the decision of _which_ criteria are used to rate
and categorize wallets, Walletbeat aims to follow Ethereum's ethos and cypherpunk values as a
guiding principle. See [Vitalik's blog post on wallets](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2024/12/03/wallets.html)
and the [Walletbeat FAQ page](../faq/) for more details.

---

Walletbeat is committed to transparency. Contributors reveal their affiliation and / or shares in
wallet companies. It is an [open-source project](https://github.com/walletbeat/walletbeat) licensed
under the Free and Open-Source MIT license.

Discussions are held on the [Walletbeat Farcaster channel](https://warpcast.com/~/channel/walletbeat).

If further funding becomes a necessity in the future, Walletbeat aims to raise funds through
retroactive funding, ecosystem grants, and individual donations. Walletbeat will then update
the above list to document the date, source, and amount of such funding.

---

Walletbeat refuses and will continue to refuse funding from wallet-related entities.

Walletbeat however was originally created and is currently hosted by [Fluidkey](https://fluidkey.com/),
an incorporated company in Switzerland with a wallet offering. In order to maximize credible
neutrality, Walletbeat's long-term ownership goal is to become an independent DAO or foundation
(similar to L2Beat) once Walletbeat reaches a higher level of maturity and a broader set of regular
contributors. Until this is achieved, Walletbeat will not list nor rate wallet software from Fluidkey.

---

Wallets listed on Walletbeat do not represent endorsements and are for informational purposes only.

If you find that something is wrong, please help Walletbeat by
[contributing](https://github.com/walletbeat/walletbeat)!
