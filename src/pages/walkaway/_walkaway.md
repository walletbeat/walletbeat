---
title: Walkaway Test
description: 'The walkaway test checks whether an Ethereum wallet keeps working, and keeps its users in control, if its team and servers disappear.'
---

[Vitalik Buterin wrote](https://x.com/VitalikButerin/status/2010621884811845708) that "Ethereum itself must pass the walkaway test."
Applications built on Ethereum should work like tools: once users have them, they keep working, with or without their developers.
**Ethereum wallets should meet the same standard.**

A self-custodial wallet gives users their keys, yet many wallets still depend on their team to keep working.
The walkaway test asks a simple question: if the team stopped working on the wallet tomorrow and shut down its servers, could users still use it safely and remain in control of their account?

Wallets are long-lived infrastructure.
Users should not depend on a wallet team's availability, funding, or goodwill to keep control of their funds.
