#### Tracks - Walletbeat response

One of the things Walletbeat didn't expect is how software wallets rating methodology grew outside of the original list we promised. Our rating methodology grew, which is a good thing. Walletbeat cares about how detailed our methodology is. Added new attributes like duress resistance, scam prevention, multi address privacy, permissions management. Also grew attributes like transaction legibility - now tracking ERC-7730 & ERC-8213 implementations. As the ecosystem grows, we should expect Walletbeat's rating methodology growing too.

Having that said, contributors focus was on making Walletbeat's software wallet rating methodology to be stable. Data collection, and hardware wallet rating methodology rating methodology was set aside, in which, Walletbeat plans to accomplish in the following weeks / months pushing for launch.

For legitimacy, Walletbeat is also trying to cover this through multiple ways:

- Already secured endorsement & funding from other parts of the ecosystem. Recently, Walletbeat participated in the Giveth funding and secured additional funding.
- Walletbeat recently surpassed 1k followers on X, which we also plan to focus on growing post-launch. This is needed for Walletbeat to succeed, as wallets won't change if no one cares about what Walletbeat says about them.
- Walletbeat team also attended the recent Berlin Blockchain week and presented on multiple occasions:
  - Neocypherpunk summit - joined a panel discussing privacy in Web3 and hosted a small session discussing Ethereum wallets privacy.
  - Ethereum day - Spoke at the conference about CROPS in wallets.
  - Dappcon - Hosted a workshop discussing & pushing Ethereum standards like ERC-7730 (clear signing) & ERC-8213 (Calldata & EIP-712 digest) helps in improving user experience & security.

### Key risks & challenges mentioned - Walletbeat response

Out of the four risks & challenges mentioned in the previous proposal, 2 major challenges were / are being faced by Walletbeat:

1. Walletbeat fails to reach ecosystem recognition critical mass.
   Walletbeat aims to be on par with L2BEAT's ecosystem recognition. For that to happen, it will take time. As mentioned before, the contributors have been focused on making the software wallet rating methodology stable. We would love to mention that we will have a strong post-launch plan for Walletbeat to attain ecosystem recognition. That includes collaborating with other projects (Cyfrin, Shefi) and initiatives (Kohaku), having a stronger social media presence, and attending conferences to talk about Walletbeat.
2. Walletbeat launch timeline slips due to contributor constraints
   One of the main challenges of Walletbeat launching is contributor fall-outs. Out of the six original contributors who were originally part of the launch, only 2 have been consistently contributing. With some contributors, from time to time, adding inputs. Nonetheless, over the past few months, Walletbeat now has a set of contributors dedicating consistent time for the project's launch and mission.
