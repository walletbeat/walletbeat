### Ethereum Foundation funding — APPROVED

**50,000 USD grant approved**, covering through end of year. Not yet disbursed. We had asked for 90,000 covering further into 2027; EF chose a shorter window. Two readings:

- The stated rationale: EF does not want Walletbeat to depend on them completely, so the smaller amount is a forcing function to seek funding elsewhere (which we are doing).
- A more optimistic framing: the 90,000 target was time-split to cover into 2027; the 50,000 matches the per-time-adjusted amount required to hit all milestones up to 31 January 2027. So it can be read as the full target on a time-adjusted basis.

Grant milestones:

- **Milestone 1 — 16 Oct 2026:** Top-5 software wallet ratings finished, all pre-launch issues closed, clear signing relaunch, UI improvements (legibility, responsiveness, polish).
- **Milestone 2 — 30 Nov 2026:** Formal launch on walletbeat.eth, donation page live, Devcon Mumbai talks/workshops/booth delivered.
- **Milestone 3 — 31 Jan 2027:** Progress slider live on wallet pages, ≥2,000 followers on X, net-new additions to the impact log.

On the impact-log requirement: Fredrik (one of the two EF members in the meeting) is extremely insistent on _visually evident impact_ — a log of the effects Walletbeat has had on the wallet ecosystem. Two ways to satisfy it:

- A draggable **progress slider** on each wallet page showing how a wallet's rating evolved.
- A **static impact log** page listing known changes wallets made in response to Walletbeat (a "prologue" version of the slider). Polymutex considers the static page the better short-term fulfillment.

The broader message: Walletbeat must not just rate wallets, it must demonstrably _cause_ changes in the ecosystem.

### Kohaku CLI network audit

Polymutex has been building a **customized harness** to test Kohaku's CLI network behavior. The harness had to be customized because of Kohaku's Tor integration, which requires a patched version of the Tor.js library allowing instrumentation from outside the process. Polymutex is trying to make it as generic as possible for other wallets doing the same type of Tor integration, though it's not fully reusable.

**Key finding:** Kohaku does **not** perform Tor **circuit isolation** — the property that each request goes through a different exit point (different IP from the target's perspective). This is critical for anti-correlation when a wallet holds multiple addresses, which is always the case when doing private transfers. Polymutex tweeted about it; it "blew up" in Zcash circles. Zcash wallets do have circuit isolation as a design goal (Zooko, a Zcash Foundation founder, shared a Google Doc of design guidance given to Zcash wallets, which included Tor circuit isolation).

**Zcash as a funding/expansion opportunity:** Lucila suggested the Zcash ecosystem could be a great source of income via a community grant. Zooko has already told polymutex they have lots of funding and would be happy to be part of Walletbeat. Polymutex had declined so far for bandwidth reasons (full-steam ahead on launch), but agrees Zcash is second to Ethereum in value alignment and much of the methodology would overlap — "the next best one". Lucila to research Zcash grant sources and whether a timeline from January would work. First step: identify what grant sources exist within Zcash.
