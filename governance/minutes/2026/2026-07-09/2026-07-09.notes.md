### **High-Level Summary of the Call**

The meeting centered on three core themes: **technical delivery, funding sustainability, and community expansion**. The team successfully recapitulated their Berlin Blockchain Week engagement, establishing a structured pipeline for video distribution and social media clipping. On the technical front, significant frontend optimizations were merged (Astro serialization fixes, UI polish, icon mapping), though data collection CLI automation remains a critical blocker for wallet data onboarding. The most urgent discussion revolved around the project's 2–3 month runway. The team agreed to prioritize the 1TS grant report to unlock follow-up funding, while simultaneously diversifying outreach to the EF ESP program, Odin Project, Gnosis, and institutional donors. DeFi yield strategies were explicitly rejected due to recent vault exploits and low risk-adjusted returns. Instead, the team will focus on building a secure donation infrastructure with NFT badge incentives to foster community legitimacy. Looking ahead, action items were clearly assigned across frontend, data pipelines, grant compliance, and event participation (Devcon India, Network School, Malaysia Blockchain Week). The meeting concluded with a clear mandate: secure funding to preserve runway, unblock data contributors, and formalize the 2026 roadmap with funding-contingent milestones.
