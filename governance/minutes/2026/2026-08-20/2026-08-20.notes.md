### Kohaku CLI

- It exists and is published by Kassandra; it's a bit difficult/different to test. <https://github.com/walletbeat/walletbeat/issues/1111>
- Polymutex tried it for network traffic analysis. Complicated for two reasons: (1) it's a **CLI**, whereas previous wallets were mobile apps or browser extensions that run inside a larger shell (browser / Android emulator) where you can proxy system-wide or browser-wide; a standalone CLI can't be proxied that way. (2) It uses **Tor** — great for privacy, but the encryption happens before traffic leaves the app, so you have to hook deep inside the wallet to capture pre-Tor traffic.
- Polymutex built a small **middleware** and logic for verifying that it catches all traffic and can attribute traffic to the right part of the wallet stack. Not all Kohaku operations have been run yet, but it's a possible solution. No requests here — just progress. The difficulty is a good sign: wallets are starting to care about privacy.
