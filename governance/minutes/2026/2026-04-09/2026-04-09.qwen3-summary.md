### Segment E: ETHCC Workshop Feedback

- **Topic:** Results from the workshop **Hester** conducted at EthCC.
- **Purpose:** To validate the methodology against community feedback and assess sentiment.
- **Problems & Solutions:**
  - **Observation:** The workshop generated no controversy, which is viewed as a positive indicator that the methodology is sound.
  - **Data Point:** The **"Stage Spread"** metric was highlighted (difference between max and min group rating for an attribute). **Source Code Availability** had a high spread, indicating significant community disagreement on its importance compared to other attributes.
  - **Observation:** The format was engaging, but security attributes are too numerous for a single session.
  - **Solution:** Future workshops should focus on narrower topics (e.g., Privacy or Transparency) to allow participants to grasp the full context.
- **Unresolved Questions:** How to track and publicly disclose the list of wallet teams who attended the workshop (per Communications Policy).
- **Action Items:**
  - **Hester:** Compile and submit the list of attending wallet teams for public disclosure.
  - **Team:** Plan similar focused workshops for future conferences (Brew, etc.).
