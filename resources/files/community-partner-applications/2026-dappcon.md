## Theme Alignment & Contribution

### Proposed Contribution: We offer physical space for communities with great ideas. How do you plan to utilize Dappcon to engage your members or the wider ecosystem? (e.g., hosting a workshop, a dedicated meetup at our venue, a community lounge activation, or a specific content track).
Workshop: Hands-on technical sessions and interactive builds

- Session Title: Setting the bar on Ethereum wallets
- Abstract of your talk:

A hands-on workshop that breaks down what actually makes an Ethereum wallet “good”, and how standards shape interoperability across the ecosystem.

Participants will use the Walletbeat testing playground to evaluate real wallets across key categories including transaction simulations, signatures, scam alerts, and EIP support: highlighting how these features directly impact user experience.

We will also do a deep dive into recent EIPs like EIP-7730 (structured data signing) and EIP-8213 (calldata digest), and how they aim to improve wallet user experience, safety, and standardization.

### Past Partnerships: Briefly list any other conferences your community has partnered with and what the outcome was.
Walletbeat has received prior funding from the Ethereum Foundation, including a grant through the Pectra Proactive Grant Round and a larger grant as part of the EF's Trillion Dollar Security initiative.

This supported continued evaluation work: filling data gaps across existing listings, onboarding new wallets and improving the evaluation framework. Every contribution helps keep Walletbeat comprehensive, current, and useful for Ethereum users.
