We went around @EFDevcon asking people just that. 🎤

Chirag from @ethnimbus on wallets relying too much on trusted RPC providers.

---

Most wallets talk to Ethereum through a centralized RPC provider.

When your wallet shows your balance, transaction history, or contract state, it's trusting that provider to return the right data.

The problem?
Most wallets have no way to check.

---

That's where light clients come in.

They let wallets verify chain data on their own, without users having to run a full node.

---

At @walletbeat, Chain Verification is one of the attributes we assess under Self-Sovereignty.

We look at whether wallets verify the chain data they receive, instead of just trusting the RPC provider.
