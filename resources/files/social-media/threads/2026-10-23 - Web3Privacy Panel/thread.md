The Cypherpunk Congress by @web3privacy is happening at Mumbai, as part of @EFDevcon week

And Walletbeat is curating and moderating the panel "Privacy's last-mile problem: Wallets" 👇

![Polymutex](./polymutex_cypherpunk_congress_2026.jpg)

---

Privacy is finally being integrated in web3 wallets. 

It's a long road, and there are many practical hurdles left to solve. 

In this panel, we'll discuss what several wallets are doing to solve privacy's last-mile problem. 

---

From onchain privacy to network-level privacy, Tor integration, address and amount correlations, private wallet recovery, and dealing with the plethora of intermediaries learning of your transaction before it gets onchain.

Featuring:
- @rsquare (Safe)
- @LucemansNL (Ethereum Foundation)
- @apoorveth (WalletChan)
- And more!

@polymutex will be representing Walletbeat and moderating the panel 🌸

---

Walletbeat is an open source database that grades wallets based on CROPS values.

The role of Walletbeat is to push the Ethereum wallet ecosystem forward, because Ethereum values only matter if users actually experience them.
