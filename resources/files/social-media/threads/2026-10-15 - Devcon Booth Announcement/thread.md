Walletbeat is coming to Mumbai for @EFDevcon, and we have our own booth.

Come see how wallets get rated, what the data means, and what wallets have changed because of it.

![Walletbeat Booth](./walletbeat_booth.jpg)

---

What’s happening at the booth:

🌸 Scheduled sessions on Walletbeat’s attributes and how each one maps to CROPS values (censorship resistance, open source, privacy, security).

🌸 A data entry workshop for anyone who wants to help fill in wallet data.

🌸 A deep dive into our website and how to interpret its data.

🌸 Tell us which wallet you use and get a random fact about it.

---

The Wallet Confession Booth 🤫

What’s the worst experience you’ve had with your wallet?
What’s one feature you wish your wallet had?
Do you actually trust your wallet?

---

We’ll also share the impact so far: what wallets have done as a result of Walletbeat’s evaluations. 

And we’ll go through the current industry initiatives that affect wallet development (Clear Signing, Kohaku, frame transactions, encrypted mempools) and how Walletbeat can help apply pressure on the social layer.
