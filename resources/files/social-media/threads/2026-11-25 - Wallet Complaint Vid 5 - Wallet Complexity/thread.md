What's your number one complaint about wallets?

We went around @EFDevcon asking people just that. 🎤

@0xTimofey from @CertiK on wallets getting too complicated.

---

Swaps, staking, trading, and more.

Wallets are turning into super apps.

Having everything in one place can be convenient. But sometimes, users just want to send and receive money.

---

A good point raised here: all these extra features can distract users from what they opened their wallet to do.

More features don't always mean better UX, especially when the basics get buried.

---

Wallets offering more products isn't a bad thing.

But should these features be front and center by default?

All-in-one wallet, or a minimal wallet that does the basics well?

Let us know 👇
