➡️ Beyond Clear Signing: What Else Should Wallets Do? by @0xmattmatt

🗓️ Wed, Nov 4
⌚ 15:30 – 15:37
📍 Jasmine Stage 4

![Matt Lightning Talk](./matt_lightning_talk.jpg)

https://app.devcon.org/schedule/beyond-clear-signing-what-else-should-wallets-do

---

Wallets are where users make critical decisions about what they authorize.

Clear signing, scam alerting, transaction simulation, and enforceable transaction constraints approach that problem from different angles: helping users understand, evaluate, and ultimately constrain what they authorize.

@0xmattmatt maps these approaches as complementary ways to protect user intent, then highlights the challenge they share: None of them protect users until they make it through the access layer and into real wallets.

➡️ Websites that outlast us: dependency minimalism and the walkaway test applied to the frontend stack by @darryl__yeo

🗓️ Wed, Nov 4
⌚ 16:10 – 16:17
📍 Lightning Stage

![Darryl Lightning Talk](./darryl_lightning_talk.jpg)

https://app.devcon.org/schedule/websites-that-outlast-us-dependency-minimalism-and-the-walkaway-test-applied-to-the-frontend-stack

---

Walletbeat asks wallets to embody Ethereum values, so its maintainers turned the same scrutiny on the website that publishes its ratings.

Using the site's static build pipeline as a case study, @darryl__yeo shows how principles of dependency minimalism and the walkaway test can help devs intentionally build resiliency, transparency, and durability into web development and publishing workflows.

📍 Lightning Stage

![Polymutex & Matt Lightning Talk](./polymutex_matt.jpg)

---

In this talk, @polymutex and @0xmattmatt introduce Walletbeat as a public good that aims to shine a light on CROPS' last mile: the access layer.

Walletbeat aims to improve the wallet ecosystem through open, transparent, and evidence-based evaluations. 

Pushing CROPS to wallets, we ensure Ethereum values make it all the way to users.
