What's your number one complaint about wallets?

We went around @EFDevcon asking people just that. 🎤

@_devisha from @Keycard_ on making hardware wallet purchases more anonymous.

---

You buy a hardware wallet to protect your assets.

But to buy one, you often hand over your full name, phone number, email, and home address.

What happens when that data leaks?

---

A leaked hardware wallet customer list is a list of likely crypto holders, along with where they live.

That opens the door to targeted phishing, social engineering, and even physical attacks.

---

Privacy shouldn't start when you set up your wallet. It should start when you buy one.

Hardware wallet makers should collect less personal data and offer more private ways to buy their devices.
