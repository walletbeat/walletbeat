Blind signing has always been an industry problem. It has been one of the cause of stolen funds, wallet drainage, and hacks.

In fact, the $1.5B Bybit exploit highlighted just how dangerous blind signing can be.

Clear signing is one step forward in solving this.

---

Clear signing introduces a human readable transaction to users, instead of signing raw calldata.

Rather than trying to understand the raw calldata, clear signing shows you what the transaction is really all about.

[Photos of raw calldata and ERC-7730 side by side] 

---

While clear signing helps in improving user experience, the real challenge is ecosystem adoption. Standards are helpful, but only when adopted. Useless when not.
