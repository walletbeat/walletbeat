Blind signing has always been an industry problem. It has been one of the cause of stolen funds, wallet drainage, and hacks.

Matter of fact, $1.5 billion dollars got stolen from Bybit due to blind signing. 

Clear signing is one step forward in solving this.

---

Clear signing introduces a human readable transaction to users, instead of signing raw calldata.

Instead of trying to understand the raw calldata, clear signing shows you what the transaction is really all about.

[Photos of raw calldata and ERC-7730 side by side] 

---

While clear signing helps in improving user experience, the real friction is getting the ecosystem to adopt them. Standards are helpful, but only when adopted. Useless when not.
