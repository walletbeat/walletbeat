What's your number one complaint about wallets?

We went around @EFDevcon asking people just that. 🎤

Mahyar from @Tele_Swap on the high fees wallets charge for in-wallet swaps.

---

In-wallet swaps save you a few steps.
No need to visit a DEX, connect your wallet, and swap.

But that convenience often comes with an extra fee.

---

The bigger question: do users even know how much they're paying?

Network fees, protocol fees, and wallet fees are all different.

Wallets should show each one clearly before you sign.

---

At @walletbeat, Fee Transparency is one of the attributes we assess under Transparency.

We look at whether wallets show a full fee breakdown, so users know what they're paying and who they're paying.
