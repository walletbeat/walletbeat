@jwmeyer from @sigp_io on improving wallet UX without giving up security.

---

Account abstraction opens up a lot of UX improvements for wallets:

→ Transaction batching
→ Gas sponsorship
→ Flexible account permissions

But how do wallets ship these safely?

---

Take transaction batching.

Users can do multiple actions with fewer signatures. But they still need to understand exactly what they're approving.

Better UX shouldn't come at the cost of informed consent.

---

Security is part of a good wallet experience, not something UX has to trade away.
