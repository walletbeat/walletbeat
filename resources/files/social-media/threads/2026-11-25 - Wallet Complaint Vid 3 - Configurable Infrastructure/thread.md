What's your number one complaint about wallets?

We went around @EFDevcon asking people just that. 🎤

@iamwehi on letting users configure their own infrastructure.

---

You hold your keys.
But your wallet still depends on outside services to work.

What happens when one of those services blocks you?

---

In this case, the @iamwehi was blocked from Safe and wanted to run their own Safe Transaction Service.

The problem?
The service can be self-hosted, but the wallet didn't let them use their own instance.

---

Wallets should let users choose the infrastructure they rely on, including infrastructure they run themselves.

Users shouldn't lose access to their wallet because a service decided to block them.

At @walletbeat, self-sovereignty is one of the core values we assess.
